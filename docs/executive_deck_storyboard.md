# Five-Slide Executive Storyboard

Use this as the structure for a PowerPoint or PDF presentation.

## Slide 1: Ontario Workforce Snapshot

Claim: Ontario employment is higher than in 2021, but sector conditions are uneven.

Proof objects:

- KPI strip: employment, unemployment rate, labour force, participation rate, YoY employment change
- Small source note: Statistics Canada Tables 14-10-0287-01 and 14-10-0355-01

Speaker note:

> This project uses public monthly Statistics Canada data to create a focused workforce dashboard for Ontario from January 2021 to April 2026.

## Slide 2: Employment Recovered, But Unemployment Pressure Rose From Its Low

Claim: Employment increased over the period, while the unemployment rate moved down after 2021 and then rose again after 2023.

Proof objects:

- Employment trend line
- Unemployment rate trend line
- One annotation on April 2026: employment 8,247.9 thousand, unemployment rate 7.5%

Speaker note:

> The headline labour market looks stronger than in early 2021, but the unemployment rate shows that employment growth alone does not remove labour market pressure.

## Slide 3: Long-Run Sector Growth Is Concentrated

Claim: A small group of sectors accounts for much of the employment growth since January 2021.

Proof objects:

- Bar chart: industry employment change since January 2021
- Highlight health care, professional services, accommodation and food, retail, and information/culture/recreation

Speaker note:

> This is where the workforce planning story becomes more useful than the headline employment number.

## Slide 4: Latest Year-Over-Year Signals Are Mixed

Claim: The latest year-over-year movement shows current pressure in some sectors despite long-run growth elsewhere.

Proof objects:

- Diverging bar chart: latest YoY industry change
- Positive callout: transportation and health care
- Monitoring callout: education, public administration, professional services, accommodation and food, manufacturing

Speaker note:

> This slide shows why a dashboard should include both long-run and latest-period views.

## Slide 5: Workforce Planning Implications

Claim: The dashboard helps identify where deeper policy or program questions should be asked.

Proof objects:

- Three implication blocks:
  - Pipeline support: health care and care economy
  - Monitoring: education, public administration, professional services
  - Next analysis: occupation or regional breakdown

Speaker note:

> The project does not claim causality. It creates a defensible screening tool for workforce planning conversations.
