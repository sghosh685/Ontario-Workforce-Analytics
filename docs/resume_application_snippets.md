## Interview Answer

> I built the project to close the gap between general data experience and labour market analytics. I used official Statistics Canada data, cleaned it in Python, created fact and dimension tables, documented DAX measures, validated the totals, and produced a dashboard and policy brief. The main finding was that Ontario employment is above 2021 levels, but the sector story is uneven: health care and professional services show strong long-run gains, while the latest year-over-year view shows softness in education, public administration, professional services, accommodation and food, and manufacturing.
